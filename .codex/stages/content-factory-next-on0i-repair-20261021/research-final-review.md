## Resolved must-fix findings

### R1 / P1: a nested passage capture can outlive its parent and repeat a completed root writer

Resolution: closed. Final `intake.service.ts:2317` validates both immediate parent and root using per-branch cycle guards. `assertResearchSourceWriterAvailable:2477` checks persisted continuation for standalone roots and native UUID `continuationStatus`; `run:719` invokes it before preparation, and `beforeWrite:738` invokes it again before the existing root claim. Identical completed-child replay at `run:713` remains before this guard. The pinned-clock regression at `tests/intake.research-reuse.test.cjs:425` proves the expired-parent refusal and, independently, the terminal-root fence after claim expiry even with an available parent. Its pre-fix failure and final success were read in `research-ancestry-controlled-red.log` and `research-ancestry-native-focused-green.log`.

Evidence on the pre-correction source: `intake.service.ts:2282` validates `sourceSnapshotKey` availability but ignores `parentSnapshotKey` and the root's persisted continuation state. `run:697` shares the root's continuation claim, but `run:714` and `beforeWrite:728` rely on a claim whose TTL begins before writer completion. `run:719` and `run:743` refresh the root snapshot TTL when the root itself is written. Thus the root snapshot can remain available after its original claim expires.

Concrete timeline in seconds: R is created at 0; B is derived from R at 10; the original R writer claims at 30 (claim expiry 3630), completes at 50 (R now expires at 3650 with a completed continuation); C is derived from still-live B at 60 (C expiry 3660). At 3635, B and the claim have expired but R and C remain. Reading C succeeds under the root-only check; C has no continuation of its own; running C can claim R and start another writer. The same persisted-state issue applies to native UUID roots through `readResearchSnapshot().continuationStatus`.

Pre-fix impact: a completed or uncertain consequential write could run again under a descendant selection after expiry of the claim. Classification: must-fix, resolved. Confidence: high. The reviewer inspected source and existing evidence without executing acceptance.

Suggested correction: validate the immediate parent chain as well as the root, and consult persisted root continuation state on capture/admission and again immediately before the writer. Preserve the identical completed child result replay. Add a pinned-clock regression covering the concrete timeline and both `res_` and native UUID roots. Expected value: one writer remains the effective boundary through TTL changes. Tradeoff: descendants become unavailable when their captured ancestry expires, which is the approved lifetime contract.
