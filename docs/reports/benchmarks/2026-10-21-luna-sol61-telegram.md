correctly attributes the pledge to a community rebroadcast and explains reset
conditions; Luna had overstated official attribution. Sol nevertheless changes
the source's age18-or-older into strictly older-than18, uses awkward phrasing
and omits some source coverage. It has no URL in its prose, which the actual
at-most-one-link rule permits but weakens source verification for the reader.

Sol case2 actually explains Composer in its body and respects two bold spans,
unlike Luna, but inherits the common core's missing reset mechanics and leaves
internal research-status language in the post. Both Sol drafts require editing.
No draft is silently substituted for an accepted saved application post.

The existing rubric and previous Luna scores are unchanged. Root is the same
unblinded judge, with one observation per case/model and no paid evaluator.
Different reasoning token counts do not prove equal internal effort. The
scores are editorial judgments, not a statistically stable ranking.
